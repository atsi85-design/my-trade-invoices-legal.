Who this policy covers

This policy describes the My Trade Invoices Android application, developed under the Hart Business Apps brand. The legal publisher name must be confirmed before public launch.

Information you enter

You can enter your business name, ABN, contact and bank payment details, client names and contact information, item descriptions, invoices, quotes, GST settings and optional logos or work photos. This information is saved within the app's local storage on your device.

How information is used

The app uses saved records to create and manage quotes, invoices, PDF documents and local reports. We do not operate a separate cloud account or our own server to receive these invoice records in this test version.

Google Play subscription

Google Play handles monthly subscription purchases, renewals and cancellation. The app checks your purchase status through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment information according to Google's own policies. The developer does not receive your full card details from this app.

Photos, PDFs and sharing

Photos and logos are selected by you. When you export a PDF, the app saves it in your device's Downloads folder. When you use Email PDF or Share PDF, you choose a third-party app to receive and send that document. The recipient and the chosen app may retain copies according to their policies.

Backups and security

The app stores its main records in Android's app-specific local storage. You can export a JSON backup, which may include client details, invoices, photos and payment information. Backup JSON files are not encrypted. Keep them in a secure place and do not share them publicly. The app does not enable Android's automatic backup for its internal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your saved records remain on your device until you remove records, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. To clear local information, open Android Settings → Apps → My Trade Invoices → Storage → Clear data. This does not remove PDFs already saved outside the app or backups you exported. To remove those files, delete them separately. Canceling your subscription does not automatically delete saved records.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data handling change. For questions about privacy, contact hartbusinessapps@gmail.com.
